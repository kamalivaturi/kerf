In short

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking technology. It keeps one item in your browser’s local storage, and only if you press the theme button. Because of that, there is no cookie banner and nothing to accept.

What this site stores

kerf-theme, local storage, first party (not a cookie)Remembers the light or dark theme you chose, so the page opens the way you left itOnly when you press the theme button; on later visits the page reads it back. It stays until you clear this site’s data or press the button again.No, see below

The value is the single word light or dark. It is never sent to the project, to GitHub or to anyone else.

Why there is no consent banner

  • EU and UK: the ePrivacy rules (Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and, in the UK, regulation 6 of PECR) require consent to store or read information on your device, except where it is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly asked for. A display preference you set yourself by pressing a button, kept only on your device and used only to apply that choice, fits that exception in our reading. Regulator guidance, for example the Article 29 Working Party’s Opinion 04/2012, accepts user-interface preferences of this kind. We use nothing else: no analytics, no advertising, no social widgets, no embedded video or maps.
  • GDPR, DPDP Act (India), CCPA: the stored value is not personal data about you that the project receives, so no consent is collected or needed. The project does not sell or share personal information, so there is no “Do Not Sell or Share” choice to offer. Because nothing tracks you, there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off.
  • India: there is no separate cookie-consent law. The DPDP Act’s consent rules apply to personal data a fiduciary processes, which does not arise here.

If we ever add analytics, embeds or anything else that stores or reads data on your device, this page will say so and we will ask for your permission first wherever the law requires it.

Other parties

GitHub hosts this site. When we checked on 30 October 2026, its responses for this site and for the release request set no cookies. GitHub controls its servers and may change this; see its Privacy Statement. Links to GitHub and Buy Me a Coffee lead to their own sites, which set their own cookies under their own policies.

Clearing it

Open your browser’s settings for this site and clear its site data, or browse in a private window. The page then falls back to your system’s light or dark setting.

The Kerf software

The Kerf panels and engine do not use cookies for tracking. The panels keep layout preferences in the editor’s local storage on your device. The privacy policy lists everything else the software stores and every connection it can make.
