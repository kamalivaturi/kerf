7. What the installer does

The installer adds Kerf to the Premiere Pro and After Effects plugin folders and, for Kerf FX, to the OpenFX folder, which may ask for administrator permission. It also sets up a small background helper that starts when you log in, keeps Kerf up to date unless you switch that off, and stops soon after your editors close. The uninstaller removes the helper’s login entry. Your .kerf folder is yours to delete. Current builds are not signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your system may warn you on first launch; the FAQ explains what to do.
